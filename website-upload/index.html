Advanced driving & camera
Robot & decoration

Compact DR4B: 30-inch lift travel. Cascade: 60-inch travel. Decoration does not change alliance license plates, collisions or scoring.

Drive close to an object with the claw at its height, then press Q. Raise the object over a goal and press E when the target ring turns green. Alternate pins and cups to build a stack. R changes the orientation of the held object or stack.

Rollers sit on top of the walls. Back the robot up to one and press F to cycle yellow → red → blue. Front and side approaches cannot turn them.

Hold I to run the rear intake. Back a loose pin into the rear funnel with an empty claw. It travels along the rollers, up the internal conveyor and into the front claw. Letting go stops the conveyor mid-feed; hold the key again to continue. The intake also accepts cups and can add one opposite-type object. Lower the claw fully to finish a feed; whole stacks use the claw.

Team loaders automatically fill their lower holder and raise only the upper handle when you approach. The load stays low. Face the holder and press Q to grab. The default is a red/yellow pin on top of a cup. Open Team loaders while paused to change the recipe and orientations. Changes apply to the next refill. L selects a loader while driving.

Head to head: you drive R1 on red, with one red AI and two blue AI. Each AI starts with a random role and retains its individual tendencies when its role changes once around mid-match. Defenders block opponents and periodically give space. Center specialists score and remove opposing top objects; field scorers use their alliance and neutral goals. Opponent alliance goals are protected, and center/neutral descoring stops in the last 30 seconds. Two minutes of driving; no autonomous period.

Scoring & simulator limits

Cups score 1. Visible red/blue halves score 5 on matching or neutral goals. Yellow halves score 5 on red/neutral goals with a red roller, or blue goals with a blue roller. All visible halves on the center goal score 10. A goal conceals the lowest half; an opaque cup half conceals its neighboring pin half.

Goal centers follow the diagram's tile grid. Objects use assisted nesting and simplified collisions rather than full rigid-body physics. Field artwork and robot geometry are independently drawn. This is your custom starting layout with solo scoring, not a certified competition simulation.
